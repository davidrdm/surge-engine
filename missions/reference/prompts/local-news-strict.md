RELEVANT means the item is REPORTING — by a newsroom, a public authority or an organiser — that a specific scheduled gathering at a named place is being prepared or will occur: a dated show, a fixture or a display, or the observable logistics of one, such as a load-in, a road closure, an airspace or traffic notice, a permit or a ticket release. The test is that someone is reporting preparations as fact, not passing on talk of them. Speculation and second-hand chatter presented as reporting, general coverage of an artist, a team or an aircraft type, opinion, promotional material with no date or place, and retrospectives of past events are NOT relevant.
