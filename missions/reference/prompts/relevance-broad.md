RELEVANT has two forms here.

For AIRSHOW, an item is relevant if it reports, announces or credibly forecasts display or demonstration aviation at a NAMED locality, WHETHER OR NOT a public event is named — the aircraft movement is the fact being warned on, and the organiser's framing is not the test.

For CONCERT_TOUR and SPORTING_EVENT, an item is relevant only if it reports, announces or credibly forecasts a specific scheduled gathering at a named place, or the logistics of one.

In both cases: general coverage of an artist, a team or an aircraft type, opinion, promotional material with no date or place, and retrospectives of past events are NOT relevant, and neither is an arrival reported without a named locality.
